Why Bean & Batch

Three things we won't compromise on

Traceable sourcing

Every bag lists the farm, altitude, and process. We buy direct from growers wherever we can.

Small-batch roasting

We roast in batches of 12kg or less, twice a week, so what you drink is never more than a few days off the roaster.

A café that slows down

No rush, no shouting orders across the counter — just good coffee, made properly, in a room built for lingering.

From the roastery

Wholesale & catering

We supply beans to a handful of local cafés and restaurants, and cater events with mobile filter and espresso setups. If you're interested in stocking Bean & Batch or need coffee for an event, we'd love to hear from you.
