Our story

Started with one drum roaster and a lot of tasting notes.

Bean & Batch Roastery began as a weekend hobby: roasting green beans in a converted garage and handing bags to friends. It grew into a café because people kept asking where they could get a cup, not just a bag.

Our mission

We exist to make thoughtfully sourced coffee ordinary — not a special occasion drink, but something you can rely on every day. That means paying fair prices to growers, roasting in small enough batches to stay consistent, and training our baristas properly.

How we source

We work with importers who deal directly with farms and cooperatives, prioritising traceability over the lowest price. Where we can, we buy direct. Every bag we sell lists the farm or cooperative, the region, altitude, and processing method.
